Common Documents Required at Most DLTCs

Almost every DLTC requires the following. Bring originals plus certified copies.

DocumentNotes
South African ID / PassportOriginal + certified copy
Proof of Residential AddressUtility bill or affidavit if bill not in your name
Passport / ID Photos2 identical colour photos
Completed Application FormsLL1, DL1, NCO, etc.
Existing Licence or Vehicle PapersWhere applicable
Booking / Payment ReceiptProof of payment
Ward Councillor Confirmation LetterRequired if living in an informal settlement

Forms, Codes & Important Notes

Form CodePurpose
LL1Learner's licence application
DL1Driving licence application / renewal
NCPChange of address / particulars
RLVVehicle registration / licensing
NCONotification of change of ownership
MCMedical certificate (65+ or PrDP)

Please note: Fees differ between municipalities and provinces. Some DLTCs only accept card payments. Booking systems and available slots vary. Gauteng encourages online bookings through NaTIS. Many services require FICA-compliant proof of address.
