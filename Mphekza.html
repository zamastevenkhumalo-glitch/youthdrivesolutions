About Us

ESTABLISHED 2014 • MPUMALANGA

📍 Originally from eManzana (Badplaas), Mpumalanga

Who We Are

Mphekza Security Force was founded in 2014 by Vickers Vilakazi, a former security officer. It was established as a superior in security services and other activities associated with the company.

We are consultants and contractors for all security services. Mphekza Security Force is the company that differentiates itself in being unique by providing excellent client services.

Vision & Mission

OUR PROMISE TO EVERY CLIENT

◈ VISION

To be the leading and most trusted security services provider in Mpumalanga and beyond — setting the standard for professionalism, integrity and reliability in the industry.

◈ MISSION

To deliver superior security solutions through well-trained personnel, modern technology and uncompromising service — ensuring the safety of every person, asset and premises entrusted to us.

Our Services

WHAT WE DO

01
Guarding & Patrolling

Commercial, industrial, mining or residential areas.

02
Secure Transport

Safeguarding assets in transit and providing secure transport.

03
Body Guarding

Close personal protection for individuals and VIPs.

04
Security Advice

Advisory on protection of persons, property and use of security equipment.

05
Reaction Services

Rapid response to alarms, incidents and emergencies.

06
Event Security

Safety and order at sporting, recreational and entertainment events.

07
Private Investigation

Professional investigative services for private and corporate clients.

08
Security Equipment

Installation, servicing and repair of security equipment.

09
Special Events

Dedicated security personnel for special events of any size.

10
Car Watches

Car watches and related activities in busy public areas.

Company Policy

OUR COMMITMENTS

Human Resource Policy

Our staff are well trained, and we strive to keep them on a competent level in security services to maintain outstanding service to our clients in terms of the latest technology. Our primary resource is our employees — we focus on training, developing and maintaining competent people within our organisation. Our service is our pride.

Safety, Health & Environmental Policy

Mphekza Security Force is committed to adhering to the Occupational Health and Safety Act, the Mine Health and Safety Act where applicable, and both the By-Laws and NEMA.

Employment Equity Policy

Mphekza Security Force is an equal opportunity employer and we adhere to the Employment Equity Principles.
