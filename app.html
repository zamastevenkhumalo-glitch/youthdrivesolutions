📘 Things to Know

1. What is a learner's licence and why do I need it in South Africa?

A learner's licence is a permit that allows you to drive on public roads only under the supervision of a licensed driver. It proves that you have basic theoretical knowledge of road signs, rules, and vehicle controls. It is a mandatory prerequisite before you can take the practical driving test for a full driver's licence.

2. What age should I be to get a learners license?

You must be at least 17 years old to apply for a Code B (light motor vehicle) learner's licence.

3. What documents do I need to get a learners license?

You need to bring your original Identity Document (ID) or valid passport, proof of residential address (e.g., utility bill), and two identical ID-sized photographs. If you are under 18, you must also bring a parent or guardian to provide consent.

4. How long is a learners license valid for in South Africa?

The learner's licence is valid for exactly 24 months (2 years) from the date of issue. If you don't obtain a driver's licence within this period, you will need to re-apply for the learner's test.

5. What are the categories of a learners license?

The main categories are: Code A1 (light motorcycles), Code A (motorcycles), Code B (light motor vehicles – cars, bakkies up to 3500kg), Code C1 (heavy vehicles up to 16,000kg), and Code EC (heavy articulated vehicles).

6. What is the difference between a learners license and a driver's licence?

A learner's licence is a temporary permit that allows you to drive only with a licensed supervisor beside you. A driver's licence is the full, permanent licence that allows you to drive unaccompanied on any public road.

7. How long should I wait to get my learner's licence?

There is no mandatory waiting period to apply. You can apply as soon as you turn 17. However, you are advised to study the K53 official driving manual thoroughly before attempting the test.
