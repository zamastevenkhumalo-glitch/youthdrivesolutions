Your data is stored securely on your own device (in your browser's localStorage) and is not uploaded to any external server unless you explicitly choose to share it. We use HTTPS encryption on all connections to our website to protect your information in transit. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only with:

All third-party operators are bound by confidentiality obligations and must comply with POPIA.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Object to direct marketing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator
  • Be informed if your personal information has been accessed by unauthorised persons

To exercise any of these rights, email info@youthdrivesolutions.co.za.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law. When no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.
