Refund Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Refund Policy explains when and how you can request a refund for purchases made through the YouthDrive Solutions app, website, or Google Play Store. We comply with the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA) and the Consumer Protection Act (CPA) of South Africa.

1. Our Digital Learning Content

YouthDrive Solutions provides digital learning content (the tutorial app) and may in future offer paid courses, lessons, or services. This policy applies to all such purchases.

2. Your Right to Cancel (ECTA Cooling-Off)

Under Section 44 of the ECTA, if you purchase goods or services online, you have the right to cancel the transaction within 7 days without reason or penalty.

  • For goods: within 7 days after you receive the goods.
  • For services: within 7 days after the agreement is concluded.

If you cancel during this period, you are entitled to a full refund, which must be processed within 30 days of cancellation. You may only be charged the direct cost of returning any goods.

Important exclusion: The ECTA cooling-off right does not apply to digital content that you have accessed immediately, or to services that have already commenced with your consent. By purchasing immediate access to digital content, you acknowledge that you lose the 7-day cooling-off right.

3. Defective Content or Services (CPA Warranty)

The Consumer Protection Act gives you a 6-month implied warranty against defects.

  • If the digital content or service is defective, not as described, or does not work as intended, you may return it within 6 months of purchase.
  • You may choose a repair, replacement, or full refund.
  • This right applies even if you have already accessed the content.

Examples: the app does not load, questions are missing, or the content does not match the description.

4. Direct Marketing Purchases (CPA Section 16)

If you purchased as a result of direct marketing (e.g., a sales call or unsolicited offer), you have a 5-day cooling-off period after receiving the goods or concluding the service. You may cancel the entire contract without penalty and receive a full refund. You may have to pay the cost of returning any goods.

5. Google Play Purchases

If you purchased the app or any in-app item through Google Play:

  • Within 48 hours: You may request a refund directly through Google Play.
  • After 48 hours: Contact us directly. We will assess your request in line with this policy and applicable law.

Google Play’s own refund policy may also apply. If Google issues a refund, your access to the app will be removed.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email info@youthdrivesolutions.co.za with:

  • Your full name
  • Date of purchase
  • Proof of purchase (e.g., Google Play receipt, EFT confirmation)
  • The reason for the refund request

We will respond within 5 business days.

7. Refund Processing Time

Once approved, refunds are processed within 30 days of cancellation, as required by the ECTA. Refunds will be made using the original payment method where possible.

8. What Is Not Refundable

  • Digital content that has been fully accessed and is not defective.
  • Services that have already been completed (e.g., a driving lesson that has taken place).
  • Customised or personalised products.
  • Gift cards or vouchers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at youthdrivesolutions.co.za/refund.html.
